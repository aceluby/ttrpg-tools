# Vaelport Mini-Arc: Marcel Possession And Soulforge

Last updated: 2026-05-13

This file tracks the current mechanics and story assumptions for the immediate post-`Paragon` mini-adventure in
`Vaelport`.

## Core Premise

- `Luceran Pyrel` has taken control of `Marcel`'s body after the artifact backlash fully triggered.
- `Marcel` is not gone. His consciousness is trapped in an inner dream-space and can be reached a little more clearly
  each night.
- `Luceran`'s possession is powerful but unstable.
- He wants to make the bond permanent before `Marcel` can reclaim himself or the body fails under the strain.
- The key to permanence is a `dragon-keeper soulforge` hidden in the caves and old dragon routes near `Vaelport`.

## Why Luceran Wants The Soulforge

- `Marcel`'s changeling body is spiritually flexible enough to seize but not naturally stable enough to keep.
- `Luceran` believes the `dragon-keeper soulforge` can anchor a soul into a living vessel permanently.
- He wants to use the forge to fuse himself to `Marcel`'s body and erase any chance of being cast back into the
  artifact.
- This makes the possession a fast-moving problem instead of a slow curse.

## Why Matilda Is Drawn There

- `Matilda Wraxor`'s presence near `Vaelport` is not about reunion with `Dax` or `Bernard`.
- After leaving her family, she spent years trying to purge or burn the dragon out of herself.
- Those attempts damaged or stripped away part of her soul, memory, and emotional capacity.
- She has come back because the `dragon-keeper soulforge` may be the only place capable of restoring what she broke.
- This means `Luceran` and `Matilda` are moving toward the same place for different reasons.

## Nightly Dream Progression

- Night 1: Sense
  - The party gets only a vague impression that `Marcel` is still present.
  - Dream imagery should be distant: black water, moonlight, muffled panic, and something just behind glass.
  - Reward:
    - the party learns `Marcel` is trapped, not destroyed
    - they get the first hint that `Luceran` is searching for a place beneath the coast

- Night 2: Stronger Presence
  - The connection sharpens and the party can feel `Marcel`'s fear, urgency, and fragments of thought.
  - Reward:
    - the party learns `Luceran` needs something in or under `Vaelport`
    - `Marcel` can push through one emotional truth or warning

- Night 3: Find And Speak
  - The party can finally locate `Marcel` within the dream-space and speak with him directly.
  - Reward:
    - `Marcel` can explain what he has seen of `Luceran`'s plan
    - he can name the `dragon-keeper soulforge`, the caves, or the direction of the threat

- Night 4: Confrontation In Marcel's Shape
  - `Luceran` appears within the dream-space wearing `Marcel`'s face and tries to break the rescue attempt directly.
  - Reward:
    - success gives `Marcel` more leverage over his own body during waking scenes
    - failure gives `Luceran` more confidence, speed, or control

- Final Night: Split Climax
  - In the real world, the party must stop `Luceran` from reaching or using the `dragon-keeper soulforge`.
  - In the dream-space, `Marcel` must directly confront `Luceran` for ownership of self and body.

## Waking-World Mechanics

- Each night, the party can attempt a dream-contact ritual through sleep, song, prayer, magic, or the artifact.
- Better rolls should produce stronger contact and let the dream track advance faster or more cleanly.
- Poor rolls should still give contact, but with distortion, danger, or misleading imagery.
- As `Marcel` regains dream-ground, he should earn small waking benefits:
  - a brief spoken warning
  - one resisted command
  - one stolen action
  - one visible tell that helps the party identify when `Luceran` is lying

## Body And Spirit Tension

- `Luceran`'s hold depends on `Marcel`'s body remaining viable.
- As the body weakens, `Luceran`'s spirit also weakens.
- This creates the intended balance:
  - hurting the body is one way to weaken `Luceran`
  - but too much harm risks killing, maiming, or permanently damaging `Marcel`
- The final confrontation should reward restraint, subdual, disruption, grapples, wards, and ritual interference more
  than raw damage.

## Current Story Shape

- Session 1 energy:
  - landfall at `Vaelport`
  - something is immediately wrong with `Marcel`
  - first dream-contact begins that night

- Session 2 energy:
  - the party starts tracing `Luceran`'s objective through `Vaelport`
  - `Bernard Wraxor`, `Matilda Wraxor`, or cave-linked clues pull `Dax`'s family arc into the same line of action
  - dream contact becomes clearer and more actionable

- Session 3 energy:
  - race to the `dragon-keeper soulforge`
  - real-world confrontation with possessed `Marcel`
  - simultaneous inner confrontation between `Marcel` and `Luceran`
