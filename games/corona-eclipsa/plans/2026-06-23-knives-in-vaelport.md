# 38 - Knives In Vaelport

- Game: Corona Eclipsa
- System: Pathfinder 2e
- Duration: 3 hours
- Tone: harbor intrigue, dream-haunting, criminal pressure, possession dread

## Clarifying Answers Locked In

- Session length: 3 hours
- The session begins inside `Ilyra Vestrin`'s home immediately after the party's arrival
- `Ilyra Vestrin` should feel warm but sharp, like an old-trader matriarch who has survived too much foolishness to
  romanticize it
- The first significant action after the house should come later through the `Salt Knives`, not inside the home
- `Ilyra` should steer the party toward the serpent problem because it threatens all trade, which makes piracy more
  profitable and the harbor more dangerous
- The immediate respectable lead is a strange alchemist who may know something about the serpent attacks
- The alchemist is cagey and has a hidden place of power in his basement
- That basement can support the final stage of `Luceran Pyrel`'s ritual if he gets the chance to use it
- `Harlan`'s old criminal ties are now named `The Salt Knives`
- They are angry because `Harlan` walked away from a job that got one member's brother killed
- The group should be pushed toward rescuing or confronting `Marcel`'s old fling
- That fling is expected to meet `Marcel` later that night, drug him, tie him up, and rob him
- Each dream sequence should move the party closer to understanding that `Marcel` is trapped and an intruder is wearing
  his life from the inside
- `Jin`'s dream should feature his real former slaver and the choice to forgive or attack
- If `Jin` attacks, he loses his `Cloistered Robes`
- If `Jin` attacks, his next dream after this session should be of one of `Soryan`'s prior champions who is now a god
- The `Seedy Tavern` scene should include `The Salt Knives` explaining that the girl only paid half what she owed
- They should offer to return the rest of the party's stolen goods for 5,000 gp
- The session should not be built toward one fixed ending; it should generate plenty of live leads and scene momentum

## Assumptions

- `Marcel`'s old fling has not fully betrayed him out of free choice; there is at least some mix of fear, debt, or
  coercion behind her actions.
- `Ilyra Vestrin` does not know the full truth about `Luceran Pyrel`, but she is perceptive enough to notice when a
  situation feels spiritually or politically wrong.
- The serpent problem, `Dax`'s cave trail, and the larger liveship mystery should all feel adjacent even if they are
  not resolved tonight.
- The party is in the early `Vaelport` phase, so respectability, criminal pressure, and old secrets should all compete
  for attention at once.

    - Useful pressure: `The Salt Knives` want him to know that walking away did not erase what happened
- `Dax`
    - Spotlight: hearing cave, serpent, and dragon clues starting to converge in one city
    - Useful pressure: every useful answer about the serpents might point one step closer to `Bernard Wraxor` or one
      step

- `Jin`
    - Spotlight: confronting his real former slaver in dream-space and deciding what kind of mercy he still believes in
    - Useful pressure: every choice he makes spiritually also sharpens the party's view of what is happening to `Marcel`
- `Marcel`
    - Spotlight: being violated twice in one night, once by the city and once by the intruder inside him
    - Useful pressure: the more the others understand, the harder it becomes for `Luceran Pyrel` to stay comfortably
      hidden
- `Ilyra Vestrin`
    - Spotlight: first respectable foothold in `Vaelport`
    - Useful pressure: she wants the serpent threat handled before the whole harbor learns to profit from fear

## Strong Start

- Recap:
    - Last we left off, Corona Eclipsa.... reached `Vaelport` after a strange voyage of bad dreams, serpent blood, and
      a liveship speaking in memories no one else should have. The party learned that serpent attacks have become one of
      the main reasons trade north to `Everthrone` is choking, though nobody in the city seems to know why the activity
      has risen so sharply.
    - `Paragon` spoke of "She Who Remembers," the harbor itself felt brittle and divided, and `Althea Vestrin` brought
      the group to her mother's home for a safer beginning than the docks could offer. On the way you met a few
      characters. The first was an unnamed woman of the night that Marcel promised he would visit later that evening.
      The second was not as lovely as a man came up and sucker punched Harlan outside `The Seedy Tavern`. You eventually
      make it to the Vestrin home which is a large fenced property, but seemingly unkempt. And that is where we will
      begin.
- Opening image:
    - `Ilyra Vestrin`'s home is lit like a place that expects storms and has learned to host through them. More dusty
      then one would expect from a home this large, the rooms look to be in desperate need of a cleaner. She leads the
      group to a sitting room, with several chairs and tables, she leaves and returns with a pot of tea and some
      cakes

## Secrets and Clues

- The serpent attacks are not random predation; they are patterned around something older and more intentional.
- The trade slowdown is making piracy, criminal extraction, and harbor extortion easier to hide inside normal chaos.
- `Marcel` is still present inside himself, but trapped.
- Another intelligence is crowding or possessing `Marcel` from within.
- The dreams are now strong enough that the party can trust what they are seeing even if they still do not understand
  it fully.
- `The Salt Knives` are using the unstable harbor economy to press old debts harder and more publicly.
- `Mira Sable` is in real trouble, even if she is also dangerous.
- `Glasswake Remedies` is more than a shop; its basement is a real place of power.
- `Luceran Pyrel` needs a place like that to push the possession toward permanence.
- Serpent routes, the liveship mystery, and the caves outside `Vaelport` all point toward the same deeper dragon
  history.

- The Lanes Behind The `Seedy Tavern`

    - Deeper details: this area should feel built for quick violence and quicker denial
- `Glasswake Remedies`
    - Rendering: cloudy glass, jars of suspect alchemy, salt-crusted shelves, and a room that smells like science trying
      to hide obsession
    - Deeper details: the basement should feel spiritually older than the shop above it, as if the building was grown
      over a wound in the city

### Salt Knives Knife-Runner

- Level 6 humanoid skirmisher
- Perception `+16`
- Skills Acrobatics `+15`, Athletics `+13`, Deception `+12`, Intimidation `+12`, Stealth `+15`, Thievery `+15`
- Str `+3`, Dex `+4`, Con `+2`, Int `+0`, Wis `+2`, Cha `+1`
- Items knife, sap, throwing knife, stolen satchel or purse
- AC 23; Fort `+14`, Ref `+17`, Will `+12`
- HP 58
- Speed 30 feet
- Melee 1 knife `+17` (agile, finesse), Damage `2d6+7` piercing plus `1d6` bleed against off-guard targets
- Melee 1 sap `+17` (agile, nonlethal), Damage `2d8+7` bludgeoning
- Ranged 1 throwing knife `+17` (agile, thrown 20 feet), Damage `2d4+7` piercing
- Opening Rush: In the first round of combat, the knife-runner gains a `+2` circumstance bonus to initiative and a `+2`
  circumstance bonus to damage on its first Strike.
- Shiv The Weak 1 action: Strike a flat-footed creature with a knife. On a hit, the target also takes `1d6` persistent
  bleed damage.
- Cut And Run 1 action: The knife-runner Steps and then Strikes. If the Strike hits, it gains a `+2` circumstance
  bonus to AC against reactions triggered by that movement.

### Salt Knives Dock Bruiser

- Level 7 humanoid brute
- Perception `+15`
- Skills Athletics `+18`, Intimidation `+15`, Stealth `+12`
- Str `+5`, Dex `+2`, Con `+4`, Int `-1`, Wis `+2`, Cha `+2`
- Items cudgel, hooked knife, broken bottle
- AC 24; Fort `+18`, Ref `+13`, Will `+14`
- HP 76
- Speed 25 feet
- Melee 1 cudgel `+18`, Damage `2d10+9` bludgeoning
- Melee 1 hooked knife `+18`, Damage `2d6+9` slashing plus Grab on a critical hit
- Ranged 1 broken bottle `+15` (thrown 10 feet), Damage `2d4+7` piercing
- Mean First Swing: On the bruiser's first successful melee Strike of the fight, deal an extra `1d8` damage.
- Street Slam 2 actions: The bruiser Strides and makes a cudgel Strike. On a hit, the target must succeed at a DC 24
  Fortitude save or be knocked prone.
- Hold Him Still 1 action: The bruiser makes a hooked knife Strike against a creature adjacent to one of the bruiser's
  allies. On a hit, the target is off-guard until the start of the bruiser's next turn.

- Ambush package:
  - for a quick ugly fight, use `3` knife-runners and `1` dock bruiser
  - for a nastier hit, use `4` knife-runners and `1` dock bruiser
  - have one knife-runner spend the first round trying to flee with `Marcel`'s satchel if you want the fight to feel
    messier than a straight brawl

- Threat: dream possession escalation
    - Use:
        - consequence engine rather than combat
    - Effect:
        - every dream, whether merciful or violent, lets the party see more of `Marcel`'s trap
        - choices shape clarity, but not whether the truth advances

- Threat: basement ritual pull
    - Use:
        - temptation, urgency, and hidden magical escalation around `Marcel`
    - Effect:

## GM Notes

- The session should feel like `Vaelport` violates the party before it ever fully welcomes them.
- `Ilyra` should provide calm, but not comfort without expectation.
- The dream sequences should not reveal everything, but they should make the possession theory emotionally undeniable.
- Every dream ending should show `Marcel` trapped and another presence close enough to feel invasive.
- `Jin`'s choice matters spiritually, but either outcome should still strengthen the party's read on `Marcel`.
- `Mira Sable` works best if she feels human before she feels treacherous.
- The `Salt Knives` should be mean, practical, and emotionally specific rather than generically thuggish.
- `Orsik Vale` should feel like someone who has already made one or two terrible choices in pursuit of knowledge and is
  trying not to make a third while the party stands in front of him.
