# 37 - Murmurs Before Vaelport

- Game: Corona Eclipsa
- System: Pathfinder 2e
- Duration: 3 hours
- Tone: emotional introspection, sea-worn unease, subtle possession dread

## Clarifying Answers Locked In

- Session length: 3 hours
- The tone should lean toward emotional introspection rather than overt danger
- The session should begin during the overnight after the serpent attack aboard `Paragon`
- The first dream sequence should only hint that something is wrong with `Marcel`
- Only PCs who roll well enough should get clear dream contact
- Dream interaction should use open-ended skill checks based on how each PC engages with the dream
- `Luceran Pyrel` should remain subtle for now
- If `Luceran` acts through `Marcel`, his first practical move is likely quiet book research rather than obvious sabotage
- The next day should include a practical shipboard problem
- `Harlan`'s pressure should remain emotional rather than produce a direct `Nighteyes` clue
- `Jin`'s dream pressure should show the conflict between `Soryan`'s peace and his slavery past
- `Dax` should still feel focused on finding `Bernard Wraxor`, but get no useful lead yet
- `Marcel`'s old lover should not surface yet
- The `Vaelport` harbor scene should be mostly atmospheric, showing fractures between old and new trader power
- The party should be pointed toward `Althea Vestrin`'s home and mother rather than toward `Bernard`
- `Matilda Wraxor` should remain completely offstage
- The main takeaways should be:
  - something is wrong with `Marcel`, but not yet explainable
  - shipments to `Everthrone` are slow for reasons that feel political, dangerous, and local

## Assumptions

- `Paragon` survived the serpent attack but took enough strain that the crew cannot simply relax on the final approach.
- `Luceran Pyrel` is not trying to expose himself yet; he is trying to learn, orient, and quietly prepare.
- The party reaches `Vaelport` late enough in the session that the harbor arrival works best as a strong closing image and lead-delivery scene.
- `Althea Vestrin` is willing to extend practical help if the party proves useful or at least not dangerous to `Paragon`.
- The dream sequences should feel symbolic and emotionally diagnostic before they feel informational.

## Characters

- `Harlan`
  - Spotlight: quiet emotional absence, missing `Nighteyes`, and the dread of returning to `Vaelport` after more than a year away
  - Dream pressure: a sensed presence pacing just out of sight, loyal but unreachable
  - Session use: let him feel the shape of homecoming before any actual old contacts appear
- `Dax`
  - Spotlight: anger at `Matilda Wraxor`, uncertainty about what kind of father `Bernard Wraxor` has become, and the intimidating scale of searching in `Vaelport`
  - Dream pressure: betrayal without answers
  - Session use: reinforce that finding one man in this city will not be easy
- `Jin`
  - Spotlight: the fracture between the `Gentle Flame`, restraint, and the part of him that remembers chains too vividly to stay theoretical
  - Dream pressure: peace and bondage occupying the same sacred space
  - Session use: foreshadow that `Vaelport` is morally compromised in ways he will not tolerate forever
- `Marcel`
  - Spotlight: subtle wrongness, strange fatigue, and behavior that can still pass as mostly normal
  - Hidden pressure: `Luceran Pyrel` is testing what he can see, say, and ask for without triggering alarm
  - Session use: build concern without confirming possession
- `Paragon`
  - Spotlight: quieter after the night attack, but not calm
  - Session use: behaves like a ship trying not to listen too closely to the sleeping minds aboard
- `Althea Vestrin`
  - Spotlight: competent captain holding a wounded voyage together
  - Session use: bridge the party from shipboard survival into `Vaelport` factional reality

## Strong Start

- Recap:
  - Last we left off, Corona Eclipsa.... crossed the dark water aboard `Paragon`, felt the liveship pry into old wounds, and watched each private struggle pulled into the open just enough to hurt.
  - When the pressure broke `Paragon`'s fragile balance, serpents answered the ship's distress from below the waves. The party fought them back and bought the ship one more night of travel, but sleep comes now with the sense that the sea has not finished speaking.
- Opening image:
  - The ship is quieter than wood should be after violence. Somewhere below deck, a rope knocks softly against the mast in a rhythm too steady to be accidental. Salt dries on the rails. Lantern light sways. Everyone who closes their eyes feels as though something is waiting to see how they dream.
- Immediate problem:
  - The party enters the first overnight dream sequence without knowing whether what they experience is stress, prophecy, ship magic, or something wearing one of those faces.

## Scenes

- Dream sequence: first murmurs in sleep
  - Read-aloud:
    - Sleep does not come like a fall. It comes like stepping into black water that somehow remembers moonlight. The surface is perfectly still, yet each of you feels that something moved just beneath it a moment before you looked.
  - Hook:
    - Ask each player how their character tries to move through the dream: prayer, observation, pursuit, surrender, defiance, listening, song, stillness, or some other approach.
  - Scene role:
    - emotional impact
    - important information delivery
    - meaningful player decision
  - Dream check guidance:
    - Let each PC choose a skill or approach that fits their behavior in the dream.
    - Best fits include `Religion`, `Occultism`, `Performance`, `Perception`, `Survival`, `Nature`, `Diplomacy`, or even `Athletics` if someone forces movement through the impossible space.
    - Moderate success:
      - they feel that the dream contains another trapped consciousness
    - Strong success:
      - they get a personal image tied to their arc and a fleeting impression of someone behind glass or water
    - Critical success:
      - they hear a half-line, breath, or emotional spike from `Marcel` without understanding it fully
    - Failure:
      - they only carry away unease and symbol
  - Personal dream notes:
    - `Harlan`: hears paws on wet stone but never sees `Nighteyes`; the feeling is loyalty at a distance rather than danger
    - `Dax`: sees a hand withdrawing from his reach, not pleading, simply gone; the emotion is abandonment before anger
    - `Jin`: stands in a shrine where candlelight and chains share the same altar; every flame gives warmth, every shadow remembers captivity
    - `Marcel`: if you want to roleplay privately, give him a version where the water reflects a face that smiles half a beat too late
  - GM note:
    - Do not explain the dream. Let the party wonder whether `Paragon`, prophecy, or stress is responsible.

- Morning scene: the quiet after poor sleep
  - Read-aloud:
    - Dawn comes pale and thin over a sea that looks almost embarrassed by last night's violence. The crew speaks softly, like people in a sickroom. Even gulls keep their distance.
  - Hook:
    - Let the party compare sleep, hide it, dismiss it, or probe each other gently while `Paragon` pretends not to listen.
  - Scene role:
    - emotional impact
    - meaningful player decision
  - Core content:
    - `Althea Vestrin` confirms they should make `Vaelport` by evening if nothing else goes wrong
    - crew exhaustion is visible
    - `Marcel` can seem merely tired, distracted, or a little too composed depending on how the player wants to play it

- Practical shipboard problem: damage from the serpent attack
  - Read-aloud:
    - Just after midday, a sharp crack runs through the deck and one of the forward rigging lines slaps hard enough to split skin if no one catches it. `Paragon` lurches once, not from the sea, but from something in the ship refusing to settle where it should.
  - Hook:
    - The party has to help stabilize `Paragon` long enough to keep the approach on schedule and avoid looking weak when they reach harbor.
  - Scene role:
    - meaningful player decision
    - things can go wrong
    - practical pressure
  - The round structure:
    - Stage-setter:
      - a damaged section of rigging, a balky turn from the ship, and frayed crew nerves combine into a shipboard problem that needs multiple kinds of competence
    - Good approaches:
      - `Athletics` to haul, brace, climb, or hold lines under strain
      - `Sailing Lore` or `Society` to follow shouted deck instructions properly
      - `Crafting` to lash, patch, or reinforce a failed line or fitting
      - `Diplomacy` or `Intimidation` to steady panicking crew
      - `Religion`, `Occultism`, or `Performance` to calm the emotional atmosphere if `Paragon` starts spiraling
    - Possible complications:
      - a crew hand freezes and needs direct intervention
      - a snapped line threatens to throw someone across the deck
      - `Paragon` mutters an intrusive truth at exactly the wrong moment
      - one task is physically simple but humiliating, testing whether the PCs obey ship command
      - `Marcel` solves something a little too elegantly
    - Success:
      - `Althea Vestrin` treats the party as people worth speaking frankly to when they dock
    - Failure:
      - they still make harbor, but arrive visibly strained and later than planned, which worsens the dockside mood

- Day scene: quiet talk and `Luceran`'s first research angle
  - Read-aloud:
    - The worst of the work passes, leaving the ship in that strange hour where everyone keeps busy because resting would mean thinking. The shoreline is still distant, but now it feels like an appointment no one can postpone.
  - Hook:
    - Give `Marcel` a chance to float a harmless-sounding bookstore request, and let the others decide whether to think anything of it.
  - Scene role:
    - important information delivery
    - emotional impact
  - If `Luceran` nudges book research through `Marcel`, likely subjects:
    - old `Vaelport` merchant houses
    - harbor histories and old berth records
    - ship superstitions and cursed vessel folklore
    - dragon legends tied to the southern coast
    - old cave maps, coastal shrines, or pre-kingdom ruins
  - Plausible lies or explanations `Marcel` can give:
    - "If shipments to `Everthrone` are choking somewhere, old trade records might tell us which families always profit when routes go strange."
    - "After last night, I want to know whether `Paragon` is uniquely cursed or whether `Vaelport` has a long history of ships with moods."
    - "If we're walking into trader politics, I would rather know the names people still lower their voices around."
    - "Southern coasts mean old songs and older ruins. If I'm going to perform for these people, I would rather not sound like a northerner guessing at their ghosts."
    - "If `Bernard Wraxor` has been living around caves and coastlines, local histories may tell us what kinds of places a careful exile would use."
  - GM note:
    - None of these are obvious lies. They should sound exactly useful enough to pass.

- Dream sequence: stronger second contact
  - Read-aloud:
    - The second sleep comes faster and worse. This time the dark water is not still. It is breathing. Somewhere in the distance, something taps on the other side of glass with the patience of a prisoner who has learned not to waste strength.
  - Hook:
    - Again ask each player how they engage. Reward changed approaches or deeper commitment to the same one.
  - Scene role:
    - emotional impact
    - important information delivery
    - meaningful player decision
  - Dream check guidance:
    - Moderate success:
      - they confirm the presence is not just symbolic; someone is inside the dream-space
    - Strong success:
      - they feel the presence is frightened, resisting, and familiar
    - Critical success:
      - they get one more concrete impression:
        - a hand against glass
        - a swallowed word
        - a reflexive burst of `Marcel`'s emotion
        - the sense that another intelligence is noticing them back
    - Failure:
      - the dream slips into private symbolism and yields no shared clarity
  - Personal dream notes:
    - `Harlan`: a distant animal warmth brushes past him once and is gone, comforting and painful at the same time
    - `Dax`: the feeling of being left behind sharpens into the certainty that some betrayals happen before words ever can
    - `Jin`: his shrine now has one open door and one hanging shackle; peace is possible, but not clean
    - `Marcel`: the water briefly reflects two versions of the same smile
  - GM note:
    - End this dream with uncertainty, not revelation. The desired takeaway is "something is wrong with `Marcel`" rather than "Luceran has possessed him."

- Arrival at `Vaelport`: old traders and new power
  - Read-aloud:
    - `Vaelport` rises from the coast in masts, salt-dark stone, and old wealth trying not to look worried. The harbor is too busy to be healthy and too quiet to be prosperous. Before the gangplank even falls, shouting breaks across the docks where one old-trader berthmaster is already refusing space to a newer vessel under loud protest.
  - Hook:
    - The party can watch, intervene lightly, keep silent, or simply absorb the names and factions being thrown around.
  - Scene role:
    - important information delivery
    - emotional impact
    - meaningful player decision
  - Dockside exchange beats:
    - an old-trader voice says newer interests can "berth with `Thraxios` carrion and pirate trash if they like their coin so much"
    - a newer trader answers that old blood would rather starve the city than share power
    - someone mutters about missing shipments, bought inspectors, and captains sailing half-crewed because nobody knows who to trust
    - references to the `Tattooed` should come as guarded mutters rather than clean exposition
  - `Althea Vestrin` bridge:
    - `Althea` warns that the harbor is having one of its "better arguments"
    - she points the party toward her family's home and especially her mother as a safer first social foothold than the docks themselves
    - this gives you a non-`Bernard` lead that still opens the city and its factions
  - Closing image:
    - End with the city looming, `Marcel` just wrong enough to be noticed by attentive players, and the sense that `Vaelport` has too many competing truths for any one arrival to stay simple.

## Secrets and Clues

- The dreams are not only stress reactions or ship-strangeness; there is a real trapped consciousness inside them.
- Someone sleeping near `Marcel` can come away certain that his emotional signature feels interrupted, not merely troubled.
- `Luceran Pyrel` is staying cautious and information-hungry rather than acting boldly.
- Book, map, and harbor history research are all plausible first moves for an intelligence trying to orient itself in `Vaelport` without outing itself.
- `Vaelport`'s shipping slowdown is not a simple weather or labor problem.
- Old traders, newer trader interests, and slaving or `Thraxios`-aligned money are all pressuring the harbor in different ways.
- `Althea Vestrin`'s family connection offers the party a more respectable doorway into `Vaelport` than wandering the docks blind.
- `Dax` can feel the scale of the search for `Bernard Wraxor` before he gets any real progress.

## Fantastic Locations

- Dream Sea Beneath Sleep
  - Rendering: black water, moonlight without a moon, glass somewhere in the dark, and the feeling that distance has intention
  - Deeper details: the dream should hold private symbols from each PC's struggle while also suggesting one shared hidden presence
- The Deck Of `Paragon` At Dawn
  - Rendering: salt-stiff rope, pale light, tired crew, and a ship trying to act normal after being emotionally wounded
  - Deeper details: every ordinary ship sound should feel one shade too deliberate, as though the hull is listening for what people say when they think the crisis passed
- Final Approach To `Vaelport`
  - Rendering: gulls circling wide, low stone quays, cranes and rigging against gray light, and wealth that smells more defensive than triumphant
  - Deeper details: too many captains are watching one another, cargo moves slower than it should, and argument hangs over the harbor like weather

## Important NPCs

- `Althea Vestrin`
  - Role: captain of `Paragon` and the party's first plausible bridge into `Vaelport`
  - Motivation: get her ship in safely, protect what remains of her leverage, and avoid arriving looking weak
  - Roleplay trait: clips her orders short, then glances at the deck as if checking whether the ship approved her tone
- unnamed old-trader berthmaster
  - Role: face of defensive old-harbor authority
  - Motivation: keep newer interests from swallowing old-trader control of the docks
  - Roleplay trait: jabs two fingers instead of one when making a point, like every argument used to be a command
- unnamed newer trader captain or factor
  - Role: face of rising commercial pressure
  - Motivation: force access, legitimacy, and space at the docks no matter who resents it
  - Roleplay trait: smiles too long before answering insults and smooths coat cuffs whenever anger leaks through
- `Luceran Pyrel` through `Marcel`
  - Role: hidden manipulator in a familiar face
  - Motivation: learn the city quietly, identify useful lore, and avoid premature exposure
  - Roleplay trait: if the player wants, let one response come a fraction too polished, as though `Marcel` had more time to think than he should

## Monsters or Threats

- Frayed approach aboard `Paragon`
  - Role: practical non-combat threat born from serpent damage, crew strain, and the ship's emotional instability
  - Use: primary daytime pressure scene
  - Mechanics:
    - treat as a short multi-check ship crisis rather than an enemy stat block
    - failure costs time, trust, and composure more than hit points
- The dream itself
  - Role: symbolic pressure zone rather than a literal combat
  - Use: reveals emotional truth, shared hidden danger, and the first signs of `Marcel`'s compromised state
  - Mechanics:
    - use open-ended skill checks with degrees of clarity rather than initiative
- Harbor faction pressure
  - Role: ambient civic threat rather than immediate battle
  - Use: make `Vaelport` feel politically unstable before the party meets any of the deeper players

## Treasure / Rewards

- Social reward:
  - `Althea Vestrin` points the party toward her family home and mother as a first foothold in `Vaelport`
- Information reward:
  - the party leaves the session suspecting the harbor slowdown is deliberate rather than accidental
- Emotional reward:
  - PCs who engage seriously with the dreams get sharper language for their own internal conflicts
- Hidden reward:
  - players begin to suspect `Marcel` is not just stressed, which sets up the later possession reveal without forcing it too early

## GM Notes

- Keep `Luceran` subtle enough that the player can enjoy roleplaying "mostly `Marcel`" with only a few hairline cracks.
- Do not let the second dream answer too much. Suspicion is the target state.
- Resist the urge to pay off `Dax`'s search immediately. The point here is scale and discouragement, not progress.
- `Vaelport` should feel complicated before it feels hostile.
- Best ending beat:
  - the city is in view, the harbor is divided, `Althea` offers one practical lead, and one or more party members are now privately uneasy about `Marcel`.
