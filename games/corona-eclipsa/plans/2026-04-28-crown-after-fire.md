# 35 - Crown After Fire

- Game: Corona Eclipsa
- System: Pathfinder 2e
- Duration: 3 hours
- Tone: shattered grief, public awe, uneasy triumph, voyage toward a darker south

## Clarifying Answers Locked In

- Session length: 3 hours
- The session is mostly aftermath roleplay rather than a danger-heavy follow-up
- The session opens immediately as the awakened dragon destroys the army
- `Serel` begins this session in shattered grief
- `Nyx` stays close to `Serel` and uses tough love to push her toward becoming queen
- `Marcel`'s new artifact should feel intriguing and helpful before its curse becomes obvious
- The old wizard in the artifact is charming and manipulative
- The artifact gives `Marcel` an immediate benefit:
  - he can heighten a spell using a lower spell slot through the artifact
- `Dax`'s upgraded dragon amulet should reveal itself in a triumphant moment
- `Harlan`'s personal beat should center on belonging with the group and his concerns about returning toward `Vaelport`
- `Jin`'s beat should connect to his slavery background and begin pushing him toward questioning `Soryan`
- `Willow Woods` should take about 30 minutes to 1 hour of table time
- The party should recover the silver magic they previously left with an alchemist in `Willow Woods`
- That alchemical follow-up should reveal the substance is pure `weave`
- `Everthrone` should include an official coronation where `Serel` becomes queen
- At the coronation, `Serel` announces there will be no new `Corterie`
- Instead, `Corona Eclipsa` will be named protectors of the realm
- The month of downtime should be handled mainly as a concise montage with room for optional roleplay
- Treasure should come from one big loot table rather than one separate table per PC
- The table should have 20 entries
- Rewards should feel like useful Pathfinder 2e gear around level 8 or 9
- The main lingering consequence of the last battle is political:
  - `Serel` is now queen of `Everthrone`
  - the public is in awe of her
- `Rhaenard` has fled to his manor and can fade into shadow if the players do not pursue him
- The session should end with the group boarding the `Liveship Paragon`, introducing themselves, and beginning the next
  leg toward `Vaelport`

## Assumptions

- The battle at the statue field is over before normal initiative resumes; this session begins in the emotional and
  political shockwave left behind by that victory.
- `Dorian`'s dragon rises with his essence inside it, but enough of his mortal body remains behind for `Serel` to grieve
  over it as a real loss.
- The realm is in a fragile loyalist upswing rather than stable peace: `Rhaenard`'s people are gone, but certainty is
  not yet restored.
- This session should feel like the end of Arc 4 and the clean, emotional launch ramp into Arc 5.

## Characters

- `Harlan`
    - Spotlight: reckoning with what `Vaelport` still means to him and hearing, perhaps for the first time, that the
      group assumes he is staying with them
    - Useful pressure: the closer the party gets to `Vaelport`, the more old guild fear should resurface
- `Dax`
    - Spotlight: reclaiming his dragon amulet, testing its new flight, and learning that his father's trail is alive
    - Useful pressure: triumph at the amulet reveal should turn into personal urgency the moment the letter appears
- `Jin`
    - Spotlight: seeing southern trouble foreshadowed through the lens of slavery and feeling the first real strain in
      his trust that `Soryan`'s path is enough
    - Useful pressure: if the gods are good, why does chained suffering keep surviving every kingdom's victory
- `Marcel`
    - Spotlight: bonding with the artifact, hearing the first polished voice from within it, and realizing this gift may
      already be choosing him back
    - Useful pressure: the artifact is useful enough that giving it up will become emotionally difficult before he even
      understands why
- `Serel`
    - Spotlight: moving from widow-like collapse into public queenship under immense pressure
    - Useful pressure: everyone needs her upright before she has finished breaking
- `Nyx`
    - Spotlight: grief held at knife-point, forcing `Serel` to function, and quietly evaluating whether `Corona Eclipsa`
      can survive becoming symbols
    - Useful pressure: she knows tenderness is not enough to keep the realm standing
- `Dorian`
    - Spotlight: absent presence, national myth in the making, and the wound at the center of `Serel`'s new reign
    - Useful pressure: his sacrifice solves the war problem faster than it solves the human one

## Strong Start

- Recap:
    - Last we left off, Corona Eclipsa.... held the line in the statue field while `Wilhye Pycey`, `the Corterie`, and
      `Rhaenard`'s soldiers tried to kill `Dorian` before the mountain could finish waking. Silver power filled the old
      carvings, the rider statue remembered itself, and the dragon rose at the last possible second.
    - Now the army is breaking under dragonfire, stone wings tear the night apart, and victory has arrived too large to
      feel like relief.
- Opening image:
    - Silver-lit dragons rip through the remnants of the army below, turning formed ranks into fleeing embers. Wind and
      ash whip across the shelf while `Serel` drops to her knees beside what is left of `Dorian`'s mortal body, hands
      shaking hard enough that she cannot decide whether to hold him or the ground.
- Immediate problem:
    - The fight is won. The price has only just become real.

## Scenes

- Aftermath in the statue field.
    - Read-aloud:
        - The last organized resistance below becomes panic. Men throw down banners and shields alike as silver fire
          chases them over the ridges. Above it all, the dragon that was `Dorian` wheels once through the storm-dark
          sky, and then is gone eastward like judgment given wings.
    - Hook:
        - The party has to decide who comforts `Serel`, who tends the wounded, who searches the fallen, and what they do
          with the first terrible quiet after impossible victory.
    - Scene role:
        - emotional impact
        - meaningful player decision
        - important information delivery
    - Core content:
        - `Serel` weeps openly over `Dorian`
        - `Nyx` does not comfort gently; she tells `Serel` she can grieve now, but not forever
        - the group can recover the artifact that ends up bound to `Marcel`
        - if desired, let the party take a final look at broken `Pycey` aftermath, scattered `Corterie` evidence, or
          the stunned emptiness left in the sky

- Action scene: `Marcel` and the speaking artifact.
    - Read-aloud:
        - The object feels warmer than the mountain should allow, almost eager in the hand. When your fingers close
          around it, the world seems to narrow to a single polished thought in a voice that is not yours: "At last.
          Someone with taste."
    - Hook:
        - `Marcel` must decide whether he hides this immediately, tests it, speaks back, or lets the others see his
          reaction before he understands it himself.
    - Scene role:
        - meaningful player decision
        - emotional impact
        - important information delivery
    - Artifact notes:
        - the spirit identifies itself only as an old wizard at first, never giving the whole truth freely
        - it offers a useful immediate lesson:
            - once per day, `Marcel` can cast a spell as though heightened by 1 rank while spending a slot 1 rank lower
        - curse note:
            - `Marcel` cannot willingly part with the artifact for long once he has accepted it
            - do not frame the curse as obvious tonight; frame it as unreasonable reluctance later
        - personality:
            - warm, clever, flattering, and always steering

- Descent and road back to `Willow Woods`.
    - Read-aloud:
        - Morning comes gray and thin over a road that should feel victorious and instead feels hollowed out by awe.
          Burned wreckage, abandoned camps, and wordless travelers all point the same direction: the realm has already
          begun telling the story of the dragons before you have reached home.
    - Hook:
        - The party can decide what story they are telling, what details they hide, and how much of the miracle they
          claim as theirs.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - meaningful player decision
    - Travel montage beats:
        - peasants and soldiers alike speak of a dragon that saved `Everthrone`
        - rumors have already made `Serel` sound half-sainted
        - no meaningful loyalist resistance remains on the road
        - `Rhaenard`'s people fled faster than they could regroup

- `Willow Woods`: the loyalist calm.
    - Read-aloud:
        - `Willow Woods` still looks like a place built for summer beauty, but the fear has gone out of it. Gates stand
          open. Guards bow instead of scrutinizing. Every visible badge belongs to people now speaking of the queen as
          though the word has already settled into law.
    - Hook:
        - The party gets a brief space to breathe, reconnect, and decide how publicly they want to be seen as the
          queen's saviors.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - emotional impact
    - Core content:
        - all local authority is loyal to `Serel`
        - any remaining `Rhaenard` men have deserted or vanished
        - `Nyx` starts shifting from battlefield protector into political enforcer
        - the alchemist still has the silver substance the party left behind
        - testing and observation now confirm it is pure `weave`, not merely weave-touched residue
        - allow 30-60 minutes of table time here if the group wants conversations, baths, sleep, shopping, or quiet
          decompression

- The alchemist's answer about the silver.
    - Read-aloud:
        - The alchemist handles the silver with reverence usually reserved for relics or acids. When at last they look
          up, their face is pale with fascination. "This is not infused with the weave," they say carefully. "It is the
          weave. Refined, condensed, made substance."
    - Hook:
        - The party must decide whether this discovery feels like a tool, a temptation, a sacred mystery, or a danger
          they do not yet understand.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - meaningful player decision
    - Core content:
        - the silver magic is identified as pure `weave`
        - it should feel rare enough that even experts are unsettled by it
        - the group can reclaim it here and decide who carries it going forward
        - `Marcel`, `Jin`, or `Dax` may each react to it very differently depending on how much they trust power they
          cannot fully name

- Dax's triumphant amulet return.
    - Read-aloud:
        - When the amulet settles into your palm again, it answers before you speak. Silver warmth races up your arm,
          the dragon-shape unfurls with sudden confidence, and for one bright impossible breath it climbs into the air
          like it always meant to.
    - Hook:
        - `Dax` can test the new gift immediately, celebrate it, or treat the triumph as one more sign that his blood
          and his family are pulling him somewhere he cannot avoid.
    - Scene role:
        - emotional impact
        - important information delivery
    - Mechanical note:
        - the dragon granted by the amulet now has a fly Speed
        - it cannot end its turn in flight
    - Roleplay note:
        - let this feel good before the father-letter turns that hope into urgency

- Harlan's belonging beat.
    - Read-aloud:
        - The first time `Vaelport` comes up in real planning, it lands in your chest like old debt. Before you can
          drift to the edge of the room the way you usually do, someone is already talking about what they will need
          from you there, not whether you are coming.
    - Hook:
        - `Harlan` has to decide whether he says anything about the thieves' guild, deflects, or lets the simple fact of
          being counted in hit harder than the danger itself.
    - Scene role:
        - emotional impact
        - meaningful player decision
    - Good ways to play it:
        - `Nighteyes` reinforces the feeling of pack rather than escape
        - one of the other PCs assumes his place in the next arc without question
        - `Nyx` or another savvy figure can quietly note that `Vaelport` remembers people longer than people like
          to admit

- `Everthrone`: coronation and proclamation.
    - Read-aloud:
        - `Everthrone` greets you like a city that has seen myth with its own eyes. Harbor smoke still hangs over the
          river approaches where the northern ships burned, bells ring from towers that should have been mourning, and
          every crowded street seems to bend toward the palace before anyone realizes they have started walking.
    - Hook:
        - The party can stand close to power, hide behind ceremony, or step fully into the role the realm is trying to
          give them.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - emotional impact
        - meaningful player decision
    - Coronation beats:
        - `Serel` is crowned queen of `Everthrone`
        - she is visibly carrying grief under iron self-control
        - `Nyx` stays near enough to steady, prompt, or harden her as needed
        - `Serel` publicly declares that there will be no renewed `Corterie`
        - instead, `Corona Eclipsa` will stand as protectors of the realm
        - the public response should feel awed, grateful, and slightly afraid

- Jin's quiet crisis.
    - Read-aloud:
        - In the wake of the bells and kneeling crowds, one smaller voice catches where triumph does not. A servant, a
          refugee, or a dock laborer from the south asks not whether the queen is saved, but whether the chains in
          `Vaelport` will matter to anyone now that the north is burning less brightly.
    - Hook:
        - `Jin` has to answer as a man, as `Soryan`'s champion, or perhaps as someone no longer satisfied with the same
          answer in both roles.
    - Scene role:
        - emotional impact
        - meaningful player decision
        - important information delivery
    - Core content:
        - victory in `Everthrone` does not touch southern slavery by itself
        - `Jin` gets the first clear emotional warning that the next act may force him to measure his god against human
          suffering more harshly
        - if he prays, the silence should feel thoughtful rather than comforting

- Downtime montage: one month under a new queen.
    - Read-aloud:
        - A month passes differently in a city remade by relief. Fires are cleaned, proclamations posted, courtyards
          reopen, and the shape of normal life begins hesitantly returning around a court that now says your names with
          gratitude instead of suspicion.
    - Hook:
        - Each player can claim a short montage beat if desired, but the group should feel the month move quickly
          toward its next summons.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - emotional impact
    - Downtime suggestions:
        - `Marcel` learns the artifact is always nearby when he wakes
        - `Dax` practices the short-burst flight of the amulet-dragon
        - `Harlan` grows more visibly at ease in the group even as `Vaelport` concerns sharpen
        - `Jin` spends time in temples or among workers and leaves both more restless than before
        - offer normal downtime actions, but keep the montage moving

- The queen's request and the southbound launch.
    - Read-aloud:
        - When `Queen Serel` calls you at last, it is not for celebration. Trade from `Vaelport` has slowed to a
          troubling crawl. Then she produces a letter, already worn at the fold, and places it in `Dax`'s hands before
          anyone else can speak.
    - Hook:
        - The party now has a political mission, a personal mission, and a clear choice to become the kind of people
          who answer both at once.
    - Scene role:
        - important information delivery
        - meaningful player decision
        - emotional impact
    - Mission details:
        - investigate why shipments from `Vaelport` to `Everthrone` have slowed down
        - follow the letter from `Bernard Wraxor`, who says he believes he has found `Dax`'s mother and apologizes for
          giving too little detail
        - `Rhaenard` has fled to his manor; if the party chooses not to pursue him now, he fades into shadow for later
    - Letter from `Bernard Wraxor`:
        - "Dax,
        - If this reaches you, then for once luck has remembered our name. I am sorry I could not send more, and sorrier
          still that I have spent so many years sending nothing at all.
        - I believe I have found your mother.
        - I do not write that lightly. I have chased rumors before, and bones before, and lies dressed up as hope. This
          is different. I have found signs that would mean nothing to most men, but not to me, and not to the woman I
          knew.
        - I cannot safely put the full truth in a letter. There are too many eyes in `Vaelport`, and too many hands
          willing to sell a family for coin. Come south if you can. Ask carefully. Trust slowly. There are powers moving
          through the port that stink of chains and old bargains.
        - If I am wrong, then I have dragged you toward another ghost and I will answer for it.
        - If I am right, then I have already failed you both by taking this long.
        - I will leave word where I know you were taught to look, if any of those places still belong to the living.
        - Whatever you have heard, whatever you have chosen to believe about me, know this much at least: I did not stop
          looking.
        - Your father,
        - `Bernard Wraxor`"
    - End beat:
        - the session should close with the party boarding the `Liveship Paragon`
        - let them introduce themselves to the ship, crew, and each other in this new phase
        - end on the first departure into the next act

## Secrets and Clues

- `Dorian`'s sacrifice solved the immediate northern crisis, but it also made the crown dependent on myth.
- `Serel`'s public power now rests partly on grief transformed into legitimacy.
- `Nyx` intends to help shape `Serel` into a harder ruler than she would choose to become on her own.
- `Rhaenard` is not dead; he is frightened, cornered, and now much less publicly dangerous.
- The new artifact bound to `Marcel` houses a wizard from centuries ago.
- The artifact is already exerting possessive influence before it reveals itself as a curse.
- The silver substance left in `Willow Woods` is pure `weave` in condensed physical form.
- `Dax`'s father is alive, active, and desperate enough to reach out now.
- The `Vaelport` supply slowdown is likely not a random trade hiccup.
- Southern instability and slavery-linked interests are poised to become the next moral center of the campaign.
- `Harlan`'s unfinished history with the `Vaelport` thieves' guild can become a liability or a hidden route inward.
- `Jin` is about to enter an arc where "the gods are good" may no longer feel like a complete answer.

## Fantastic Locations

- The Broken Statue Field
    - Rendering: dragonfire smoke, cracked kings, and cold wind rushing through a battlefield that no longer knows
      whether it is a graveyard or a shrine.
    - Deeper details: shattered armor glitters among white stone chips, silver residue still hums in old carvings, and
      every silence feels too large for the number of living people left in it.
- `Willow Woods` In Relief
    - Rendering: open gates, spring color, lakeside calm, and guards whose posture has loosened for the first time in
      weeks.
    - Deeper details: everyone speaks quieter around `Serel`, servants trade dragon stories in corners, and the town's
      beauty now feels like recovery instead of confinement.
- Coronation Hall Of `Everthrone`
    - Rendering: banners rehung in haste, polished stone catching candlelight, and a throne room trying to become
      stable faster than grief allows.
    - Deeper details: court faces study `Serel` for strength, study the party for myth, and study the empty place where
      `Dorian` should have been for fear.
- The Docks Of `Everthrone`
    - Rendering: river mist, scorched harbor rumors, and the smell of trade trying to restart under a new queen.
    - Deeper details: sailors speak of dragons in the same tone they use for storms, and every southbound conversation
      now carries an undernote of need.
- The `Liveship Paragon`
    - Rendering: polished hull, patient creaking timbers, and the unsettling sense that the ship is already listening
      before anyone introduces themselves.
    - Deeper details: the vessel should feel old, observant, and ready to become a character the moment the group gives
      it attention.

## Important NPCs

- `Queen Serel Aiuvarin`
    - Role: newly crowned queen and grieving heart of the realm
    - Motivation: keep the kingdom standing long enough for `Dorian`'s sacrifice to mean something
    - Roleplay trait: speaks fast when emotion threatens to overtake her, then clamps down so hard the silence hurts
- `Nyx`
    - Role: hard-edged protector, adviser in all but title, and the knife keeping softness from becoming collapse
    - Motivation: make sure `Serel` survives both grief and power
    - Roleplay trait: gives comfort like a soldier setting a dislocated shoulder, necessary and not gentle
- The Wizard In `Marcel`'s Artifact
    - Role: hidden manipulator and future problem wearing the mask of a useful mentor
    - Motivation: remain close to a powerful host, regain influence, and be wanted before he must become necessary
    - Roleplay trait: compliments before he instructs and sounds delighted whenever curiosity overcomes caution
- `Bernard Wraxor`
    - Role: absent father drawing `Dax` south
    - Motivation: reach his son at last and finish a search that may rewrite Dax's whole life
    - Roleplay trait: only present through the letter for now, but his apology should sound plain, military, and real
- `Rhaenard`
    - Role: fallen rival who may become future shadow rather than current battlefield threat
    - Motivation: survive humiliation and wait for a chance to matter again
    - Roleplay trait: still performs confidence even while running from its collapse

## GM Notes

- This session should breathe. Do not rush grief, but do not let grief become the only texture.
- The three emotional pillars are:
    - `Serel` learning to stand up as queen
    - the party realizing they are now symbols, not just survivors
    - the next arc arriving through personal hooks rather than abstract orders
- Keep `Nyx` sharp with `Serel`, not cruel. The point is that love looks like discipline when collapse is not affordable.
- The artifact should be delightful enough that `Marcel` wants to trust it before he has any reason not to.
- Treat the pure `weave` reveal as a major magical discovery, not routine treasure identification.
- `Harlan`'s beat works best if belonging is offered casually rather than as a speech.
- `Jin`'s beat works best if no one argues theology with him directly; let the wound come from reality instead.
- If players become unexpectedly aggressive about hunting `Rhaenard`, let `Nyx` or `Serel` explain that the realm needs
  stability first, but leave the choice open.
- Best final image:
    - ropes cast off
    - the `Liveship Paragon` seeming to listen
    - `Dax` with his father's letter
    - `Marcel` hearing a voice no one else can
    - `Harlan` looking south toward old trouble
    - `Jin` watching the same horizon and wondering what holiness is worth if chains still wait there
